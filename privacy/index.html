Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

MTPublisher ("the App") is a personal desktop tool. This policy explains what data the App handles when its owner connects their TikTok account.

1. Data the App accesses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives, with the owner's permission:

  • user.info.basic: display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: permission to send videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • video.publish: permission to post videos, with the caption and privacy setting the owner chose, directly to the owner's own profile.

2. How data is used

The data is used only to show the connected account and to publish videos the owner selects. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage

Access tokens and profile information are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local folder with restricted file permissions. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the App.

4. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok (and other platforms the owner chooses) through their official APIs.

5. Other users

The App does not collect any information about other TikTok users.

6. Data deletion

The owner can disconnect the App at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services) and delete the local token files. Doing so removes all data held by the App.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children and does not knowingly process children's data.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
