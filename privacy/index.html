Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

MTPublisher ("the App") is a personal desktop tool. This policy explains what data the App handles when its owner connects their own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

1. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. By connecting a YouTube channel to the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

With your permission, the App accesses:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos you select, with the title, description and privacy setting you chose.
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel's name and profile picture, used only to show which channel is connected.

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

2. TikTok

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives, with the owner's permission:

  • user.info.basic: display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: permission to send videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • video.publish: permission to post videos, with the caption and privacy setting the owner chose, directly to the owner's own profile.

Access can be revoked in TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services).

3. How data is used

Data is used only to show the connected accounts and to publish videos the owner selects. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose, and is never combined with data from other sources.

4. Storage

Access tokens, channel names and profile pictures are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local folder with restricted file permissions. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the App. The App does not use cookies and does not store any data on users' devices other than these local files.

5. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to the platforms the owner chooses (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram) through their official APIs.

6. Other users

The App does not collect any information about other users or viewers.

7. Data deletion

The owner can revoke access as described above and delete the local token files at any time; this removes all data held by the App. Stored data is also deleted within 30 days after access is revoked. To request deletion or ask a question, contact the address below.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children and does not knowingly process children's data.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
