MTPublisher
Personal video publishing tool for macOS

Publish my own short videos, from my Mac

MTPublisher is a personal desktop tool for macOS. Its owner, a single creator, uses it to publish their own short-form videos (Pokémon, fantasy and anime animations they create) to their own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own YouTube channel (Google sign-in) or TikTok account (Login Kit) on the platform's official authorization page.
  2. The creator selects a finished vertical video on their computer, reviews the caption and clicks Post.
  3. The video is uploaded through the official YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services) or the TikTok Content Posting API, with the title, caption and privacy setting the creator chose.

What it does not do

  • It does not post anything without the creator's action.
  • It does not access other users' data or accounts.
  • It is not offered, sold or distributed to the public.

MTPublisher uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.
