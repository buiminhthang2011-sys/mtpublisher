MTPublisher
Personal video publishing tool for macOS

Publish my own short videos, from my Mac

MTPublisher is a personal desktop tool for macOS. Its owner, a single creator, uses it to publish their own short-form videos (Pokémon, fantasy and anime animations they create) to their own TikTok accounts.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok account by signing in on TikTok's official authorization page (Login Kit).
  2. The creator selects a finished vertical video on their computer, reviews the caption and clicks Post.
  3. The video is sent to TikTok through the official Content Posting API, either to the creator's inbox as a draft or posted directly to their profile with the caption and privacy setting the creator chose.

What it does not do

  • It does not post anything without the creator's action.
  • It does not access other users' data or accounts.
  • It is not offered, sold or distributed to the public.
